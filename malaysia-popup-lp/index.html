About the Brand

What is Vibram FiveFingers?

Vibram FiveFingers are minimalist shoes with five individual toe pockets, a thin and flexible Vibram rubber sole, and almost nothing in between you and the ground. They are designed to let your feet move the way they were built to move.

  • Born from Vibram, Italy Vibram has been making rubber outsoles for mountaineers and athletes since 1937. FiveFingers was born in 2004 and started the barefoot footwear movement.
  • Five toes, one foot Each toe sits in its own pocket, so your toes can spread, grip and balance independently, just like when you are barefoot.
  • A model for every activity Road and trail running, water sports, yoga, training and everyday wear.
    You can enjoy barefoot shoes in every purpose.

Why Barefoot?

Have you ever faced these issues?

  • 01 Bunions & bent toes

    Narrow shoes squeeze the toes together and push the big toe out of line.

  • 02 Heel pain & flat feet

    Cushioned heels and arch support let the foot muscles switch off and weaken.

  • 03 Knee pain & weak ankles

    Heel striking sends impact straight into the joints and dulls balance reflexes.

  • 04 Back pain

    A raised heel tilts the body forward and loads the lower back and shoulders.

— Your solution is here —

Benefits of Barefoot

Healing modern aches with barefoot movement

Development of the soles and the feet overall

Build core foot strength to naturally align your body posture.

Relation to health problems

Boost blood circulation to relieve tension and chronic pain from conditions such as flat feet, bunions and more.

Relation to injury

Activate sensory feedback for faster reflexes and better foot landing. This promotes injury prevention by optimizing impact mechanics, allowing muscles to better absorb the landing force.

Normal Shoes vs Barefoot Theory

Normal Shoes

  • Posture: Heel drop

    Shifts gravity forward, causing back and shoulder tension.

  • Toe freedom: Constricted toes

    Narrow toe box squeezes toes together.

  • Sensory: Thick soles

    Block ground feedback, slowing balance reflexes.

  • Impact: Heel strike

    Sends harsh impact directly into joints.

  • Rehab: Like a “splint”

    Restricts movement and weakens foot muscles.

Barefoot Theory

  • Posture: Barefoot

    Naturally aligns spine, hips and shoulders.

  • Toe freedom: Natural splay

    Free to spread and move independently.

  • Sensory: Direct contact

    Awakens nerves for instant balance and stability.

  • Natural cushion

    Acts as a natural shock absorber for softer landings.

  • Awakens muscles

    Strengthens deep muscles, fixing root causes.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is the 30% discount on every model?

The event offers 10% to 30% OFF across the Vibram FiveFingers range. The exact discount depends on the model and colourway, and is applied at the booth during the three event days only, while stocks last.

Can I try the shoes on before buying?

Yes, that is the whole point of the pop-up. All models in the lineup are available to try in men’s and women’s sizes. FiveFingers fit differently from regular shoes because each toe has its own pocket, so try a few models and sizes and our team will help you find the pair that fits you best.

I have never worn barefoot shoes. How do I start?

Take it gradually. Begin with short walks of 20 to 30 minutes and increase the time over a few weeks as your feet and calves adapt. Build up to running or longer sessions only once walking feels comfortable. Listen to your body and rest if anything feels sore.

Do I need special socks?

No. FiveFingers are designed to be worn without socks. If you prefer socks, five-toe socks work with every model.

Can I exchange or return a pair?

Items bought at the popup booth cannot be returned or exchanged, so please try them on and check the size carefully before you buy. Our staff will help you find the right fit.

How can I pay?

Cash, QR payment and credit cards are accepted at the booth.
