Every Vivobarefoot shoe is built on three principles. A wide, foot-shaped toe box lets your toes splay for balance and posture. Why barefoot?

Have you ever faced these issues?

01

Bunions & bent toes

A big toe that leans inward, a bump at the joint, toes that overlap.

02

Heel pain & flat feet

Arches that feel flat and tired, heels that ache after a day of standing.

03

Knee pain & weak ankles

Heavy landings travel up the leg, and small balance corrections never happen.

04

Back pain

The hips and lower back spend the whole day quietly compensating.

Your solution is here

Barefoot benefits

A natural answer to modern aches, through barefoot movement.

Development of the soles and the feet overall

Build core foot strength to naturally align your body posture.

Relation to health problems

Boost blood circulation to ease tension from conditions such as flat feet, bunions and more.

Relation to injury

Activate sensory feedback for faster reflexes and better foot landing.
This promotes injury prevention by optimizing impact mechanics, allowing muscles to better absorb the landing force.

Start slowly. Your feet have been cushioned for years, so begin with short wears on easy ground and add time and days gradually, at your own pace. If something hurts rather than feels tired, stop for a few days, go back a step, and ask us or a professional.

Good to know

Questions we hear a lot

Anything else, ask the staff at the booth or message us on LINE.

How do I pick the right size?

The best way is to come to the booth and try the shoes on. Our staff will help you find the best model and size for you.

Do I need five-toe socks for FiveFingers?

You can wear FiveFingers without socks. Many people prefer thin five-toe socks for comfort, sweat and longer days, and we sell them at the booth. Vivobarefoot works with any socks you already own.

How do I wash them, and what about smell?

Most models can be washed by hand or on a gentle cold cycle with mild detergent, then air-dried away from direct sun and heat. Letting them dry fully between wears is the main thing that keeps smell away. Ask the staff about leather models, which need a little more care.

Can I run in them?

Yes, both brands have running models, like the FiveFingers V-Run and the Vivobarefoot Primus Flow. The key is to walk in them first and add running gradually, with shorter and lighter steps than you are used to.

Can I exchange or return a pair?

Items bought at the popup booth cannot be returned or exchanged, so please try them on and check the size carefully before you buy. Our staff will help you find the right fit.

How can I pay?

Cash, Thai bank QR payment and credit cards are accepted at the booth.
